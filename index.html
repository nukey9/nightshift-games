About the studio

Made after dark

Night Shift Games is an independent game studio based in Wisconsin, USA. We build for mixed reality on Meta Quest: games that use the room you're actually standing in, so the scares, the cover and the chaos all happen around you.

Our first game, Barricaded Mixed Reality, turns any room into a last stand against the undead.
