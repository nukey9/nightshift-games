Frequently asked questions

The walls or windows don't line up with my room.

Barricaded builds its battlefield from your headset's room scan. Re-run Space Setup in your headset's settings (under Physical Space), making sure every wall, the floor and the ceiling are captured, then restart the game. Scanning in good light helps.

How much space do I need?

Play in a room you've scanned and cleared of anything you could trip over or hit. You'll be moving, turning and swinging your arms, so the more open floor the better. For the Outdoor mode, use a safe, level, private outdoor space. See the safety section of our Terms of Service.

How do I play co-op with friends?

Everyone needs to be in the same physical space and on the same Wi-Fi. On the PLAY page, use the co-op box: one player hosts, the others join the nearby game, then follow the steps to line up so everyone's room matches. Co-op comes with the Supporter Pack, and up to four can play: everyone who plays together needs it, and the game offers it when you try to host or join without it.

Reloading is too hard / too easy.

Change Reloading Difficulty on the menu's PLAY page between matches (or in SETTINGS during one). The easiest setting reloads almost by itself; the harder ones make you handle every magazine yourself.

Can I replay the tutorial?

Yes — press TUTORIAL on the menu's PLAY page, between matches.

My score isn't on the leaderboard.

Leaderboards need you to be online and signed in to your Meta account. Scores are posted when a match ends. Tutorial matches, matches where cheats were used, and scores that look impossible aren't counted.

A friend can't join: it says to update the game.

Everyone in a co-op game needs the same version of Barricaded. Update the game on the headset the message names (in the Meta Horizon Store, or your headset's library), then join again. Each headset's version is shown on the main menu, under your level.

Is Barricaded free?

Yes. The whole solo game is free: every world, playing in your room or outdoors. The Supporter Pack is an optional purchase for anyone who wants to support the game, and it's what adds co-op.

What's in the Supporter Pack?

It says thanks with 19 more guns in the mystery box, a rare explosive bow drop, the Akimbo perks (a pistol, rifle or SMG in each hand) and co-op: play with friends in the same room, up to four of you. Everyone who plays together needs it. You can get it from the SUPPORT tab on the game's menu.

I have a code. How do I redeem it?

Redeem it in the Meta Horizon app on your phone (Redeem code), signed in to the same Meta account as your headset. Then open the SUPPORT tab in the game and press CHECK AGAIN, or restart the game. Codes are free and can't be sold or traded; see our Terms of Service.

I bought the Supporter Pack, but nothing unlocked.

Make sure the headset is online and signed in to the Meta account that bought it or redeemed the code, then press CHECK AGAIN on the SUPPORT tab. If it still doesn't unlock, contact us.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases, including the Supporter Pack, and refunds go through the Meta Horizon Store under Meta's refund policy. You can request one from your Meta account's purchase history. If the Supporter Pack is refunded, what it unlocked is locked again.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the game removes everything it saved on your headset. To have your leaderboard entries deleted, email us with your Meta username. More in our Privacy Policy.

Didn't find your answer?

Send us a report or an email and a real person will get back to you.
