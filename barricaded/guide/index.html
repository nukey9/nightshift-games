1. The game

Barricaded is a round-based zombie survival game for Meta Quest 3 and 3S that plays in your real room. Your doors and windows become the barricaded ways in, a ruined world opens up beyond your walls, and the dead keep coming — more of them, and worse, every round. Hold them off with guns you load and rack with your own hands, and spend what you earn on whatever keeps you alive.

  • Your Room — the room you stand in. With Multi-room on, the rooms joined to it by a door too, bought open as you go.
  • Outdoor Arena — a fenced arena set out on open ground. No room scan needed, but it does need the game's Spatial Data permission: that's how the game sees the ground and what's in the way.
  • Co-op — up to four players in the same space and on the same Wi-Fi. Co-op comes with the Supporter Pack: everyone who plays together needs it.

Clear your play space of anything you could trip over or hit. See the safety section of our Terms of Service.

2. Before you play

  • Room scan and permission. Playing in your room needs your headset's Space Setup scan and the game's Spatial Data permission; the Outdoor Arena needs the permission. Until you have what the mode needs, the menu still shows the arena around you, and the start button on the PLAY page says what's missing (Scan My Room, Allow Room Access or Allow Spatial Data) and takes you there.
  • The menu opens with the menu button on the left controller.
  • Tutorial. Offered the first time you play, and any time from TUTORIAL on the PLAY page. Between matches there's a firing range outside your window to practise on.
  • During a match the menu opens on MATCH: your health, the round, your points and kills, and the guns you carry. Restart and Stop Game are there too (press them twice to be sure).
  • Reloading difficulty (on the PLAY page between matches; it stays the same for the whole match): from Auto Reload, where guns reload themselves, through Easy and Assisted, to Full Manual, where every magazine is yours to handle. Harder settings give you a little more in return.

3. How a match goes

Round after round: the points you earn buy what keeps you alive in the next one.

  1. You start with a pistol. Grenades are free: four on your chest at the start of every round, up to six.
  2. Hold the ways in. Zombies tear the boards off your windows and climb in, or break through your walls. Every hit earns points; kills earn more.
  3. Board up broken windows with planks from the pile beneath them — it slows them down and earns points too.
  4. Spend your points between attacks: a better gun, a turn at the mystery box, perks, upgrades.
  5. Catch your breath at the breaks between rounds — and watch out for the rounds that bring something other than zombies. Full Ammo comes after every 5th round.

4. Spending your points

Hold grip on anything for sale to buy it. The price is shown on it.

  • Wall buys — the guns hung on your walls, and two mines (Landmine and Claymore). Buying a gun you already carry refills its ammo.
  • The mystery box — a random weapon each turn, from the ordinary to the very rare. Take it before it's gone.
  • The generator — nothing that runs on power works until it's started. Follow the cables.
  • Perks — drink one to keep it for the rest of the match: more health (Toughness), more damage (Firepower), getting back up on your own (Resurrection), boarding up bare-handed (Handyman), faster revives (Field Medic), and the Akimbo perks (with the Supporter Pack): with a gun in each hand, each one reloads at your belt, racks with the other hand and shoots without the one-handed spread.
  • The upgrade station — opens at the breaks after rounds 9, 14, 19 and every five rounds after. Levels 1–3 come first, a 4th after round 19 and the rest after round 29 (machine guns other than the MG 34 wait for round 29). Each level makes the gun hit harder, and more; a pistol at level 3 fires explosive rounds — for crowds, with fewer spare magazines.
  • Doors — open up more of your home (Multi-room) or the arena: more room to fight in, and more ways in.

5. Your belt

  • Two slots on your shoulders and two at your hips hold your guns; let go of a gun over a slot to holster it. Drop one and it comes back to you.
  • Grenades are on your chest. Pull the pin with your other hand (or bring the ring to your mouth), then throw.
  • Click a thumbstick for a menu of everything on your belt.
  • Keep an eye out for what the dead sometimes drop.

6. Going down

  • Stay out of reach for a moment and your health comes back.
  • Alone, going down ends the match — unless you have Resurrection: then choose GET UP or END RUN (press it twice to be sure). Choose nothing and you get up after 15 seconds.
  • Together, a teammate can revive you by holding a hand near your head before you bleed out. Stick close to each other: it helps.

7. Levels, scores and the Supporter Pack

  • Every match earns XP toward your level. Kills with a gun build its mastery (the MASTERY tab): levels at 500, 1,000, 2,000 and 4,000 kills, each a small bonus for that gun.
  • In the gunsmith (LOADOUT) every gun hangs on the racks, and a gun's attachment slots open with its mastery: grips at 500 kills, sights at 1,000, lasers and lights at 2,000, muzzles at 4,000. What a gun wears when you leave is how it comes into every game.
  • LEADERBOARD has the leaderboards — your room and the outdoor arena, GLOBAL or FRIENDS. Points earned on a harder reload setting count for more: Easy +5%; Assisted +5% in round 1, rising each round to +25%; Full Manual +10%, rising each round to +50% (both at their top from round 21). Casual matches aren’t ranked.
  • The Supporter Pack (SUPPORT tab) adds 19 more mystery box guns, a rare explosive bow drop, the Akimbo perks, and co-op: play with friends in the same room, up to four of you. The whole solo game is free. See the FAQ.

8. Playing together

  1. Everyone in the same space and on the same Wi-Fi, and everyone with the Supporter Pack (the game offers it when you try to host or join without it).
  2. One player: PLAY page → Host a game here. Their room becomes everyone's room.
  3. The others: Join the game shown, or accept the host's invite.
  4. Everyone lines up (below) — usually by itself.
  5. The host starts the match.

If someone drops out, they can Rejoin, and the others can choose to carry on without them.

9. Lining up headsets

To play together, every headset has to agree where the walls, windows and zombies are. Most of the time that happens by itself in a few seconds, and the co-op box says Lined up with the host's room. Check by looking at a window: its frame should sit on the real one.

If it doesn't line up — or things look shifted — line up yourself with a QR code or by touch. Between matches all the buttons are in the PLAY page's co-op box. During a match, SETTINGS shows the one that fits (usually Line up by QR code for a friend, or Realign for the host).

By QR code

Needs a Quest 3 or 3S on Horizon OS v83 or later. Print a code from the line-up code page first.

Step 1

Print a code at actual size (the black square 18 cm across) on plain matte paper.

Step 2

Tape it flat on the floor (where nobody steps) or on a wall at chest height, somewhere well lit. Leave it there.

Step 3 · host

The host presses Read the QR code and looks at it from a step or two away, holding still a moment, until it says QR code read. Once a session is enough.

Step 4 · everyone else

Each friend presses Line up by QR code and looks at the same code, holding still a moment: Lined up by the QR code.

By touch

Works on any headset. Pick two spots everyone can reach, at least a metre apart — two corners of a table, or two marks on the floor.

Step 1

Agree on spot A and spot B. Everyone touches the same two, in the same order.

Step 2

The host presses Set the touch spots; each friend presses Line up by touch. With an empty hand, put the controller's tip on spot A and hold the trigger still for a second until it buzzes. Then spot B the same way.

It doesn't matter who goes first. If the game says your spots don't match the host's, start again at A.

Still out of line?

  • Scan the room again (Space Setup) on both headsets, in good light.
  • Try Line up again, then the QR code, then touch.
  • The host can press Realign, and everyone lines up again.
  • Still stuck? Tell us.
