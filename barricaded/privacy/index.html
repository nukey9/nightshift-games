Barricaded Mixed Reality Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Night Shift Games · Back to Barricaded

This policy explains what information Barricaded Mixed Reality ("Barricaded"), a mixed reality game for Meta Quest, uses, why it uses it, and the choices you have. Barricaded is made by Night Shift Games, in Wisconsin, USA ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email contact@nightshift-games.com.

In short: we don't sell your information and we don't use it for advertising. The only information that leaves your headset for the internet is your match results, which go to our score server so they can be checked and placed on Meta's leaderboards, and, when you play co-op, a spatial anchor shared through Meta so the headsets in the room can line up with each other. If you buy the optional Supporter Pack, Meta handles the purchase; we never see your payment details.

Information we get from Meta

When you play with your Meta account, the game uses Meta's platform to get:

  • Your user ID: an ID Meta gives you for this game only. We use it to keep your leaderboard entries and standing, and to show your player profile.
  • Your username and display name: shown next to your scores on the leaderboards and on your in-game player profile, and your display name is also shown to the players you play co-op with nearby (in the co-op lobby and over your head in the game).
  • Your profile photo: shown next to your scores on the leaderboards and on your in-game player profile, to anyone who views them in the game. The game loads it from Meta when it shows a leaderboard and keeps it only while the game is running; it is never saved or sent anywhere else.
  • Which of your friends are on the leaderboards: when you pick FRIENDS on a leaderboard, Meta gives the game the entries of the friends of yours who play Barricaded, to show that board. The game doesn't save your friends list or send it anywhere.

We don't use any other Meta account information.

Game information

To run the leaderboards and player profiles, the game records, for each mode you play (Room and Outdoor):

  • your best score, the wave you reached, and whether that match was played alone or with others;
  • your total points, games played, kills, most kills in one match, and time played;
  • how many kills you got with each weapon (used to show your favourite gun);
  • your experience points (XP), earned across all modes, which set your level.

This information is saved in a file on your headset. Your display name, scores, these stats and your level are shown to other players of Barricaded on the in-game leaderboards and player profiles (your XP total itself is shown only to you).

Leaderboards and fair play

To keep the leaderboards fair, the game doesn't post scores itself. When you play signed in to Meta, after each match it sends the match result (score, wave, kills, time, the weapons you used, whether you played alone or with others) and your XP to our score server, together with your user ID and two proofs issued by Meta: one confirming which Meta account is playing, and one confirming that the game is a genuine, unmodified copy running on a real Meta Quest (Meta's device and app integrity check). Matches played offline are sent the next time you're online.

Our score server checks the proofs with Meta, checks the match could really have been played, and then writes your entries to Meta's leaderboard service. It keeps a running record of your standing in each mode (games, scores, kills, time played, your top weapons and your XP) so the all-time totals can't be tampered with. It doesn't keep the proofs.

Each leaderboard entry on Meta carries, with your score, the stats behind it: on the best-match boards whether the match was played alone or with others and the wave reached; on the all-time boards your standing in that mode as listed above, and your XP. That is how other players' games show your profile and level. Like the scores and your display name, these entries can be read by anyone viewing Barricaded's leaderboards through Meta's platform, not only inside the game.

Like any internet service, our score server receives your IP address when the game connects to it, to answer. The score server runs on Cloudflare.

Your room and your headset

  • Room scan. Barricaded is a mixed reality game. It uses the room layout from your headset's own Space Setup (walls, floor, doors, windows and furniture) to fit the game to your room. It is never sent to us. When you play co-op, the hosting headset shares its room's outline with the other headsets in the game (see below). The game never receives camera images (see "Line-up QR codes" below).
  • Play together on the same Wi-Fi. If you play co-op with other headsets in the same room, the headsets talk directly to each other over your local network. They share what is needed to play together: each headset's model and an identifier for it, which version of the game it runs (so headsets with different versions aren't joined together), your name (your Meta display name, or your headset's name if you aren't signed in to Meta), the outline of the shared room (walls, doors, windows and furniture), and live game information such as player positions and scores. This doesn't go over the internet and isn't sent to us.
  • Finding headsets nearby. While the game's main menu is open (or while you host a game), it announces itself on your local network so headsets nearby can find each other and be invited: your name, your headset's model, what you're doing in the game (for example "hosting" or "in a match"), and a random identifier made for this purpose when the game is installed. Anyone on the same local network can see this while the menu is open. You join another player's game only if you accept their invite.
  • Lining headsets up (co-op). To line the headsets up in the same room, the hosting headset places a spatial anchor where you stand and shares it through Meta's shared spatial anchors service, which stores it on Meta's servers so the other headsets in the game can load it. This needs your headset's Enhanced Spatial Services setting to be on; if it is off, headsets line up another way (below), and nothing is shared through Meta.
  • Saved line-ups (co-op). So headsets that have played together in a room line up again without the internet, each headset saves spatial anchors on the headset itself: the hosting headset saves its room's anchor, and a joining headset saves one of its own next to it, with where the two stand from each other and the host's anchor's identifier. These are kept only on the headset (through Meta's on-device spatial anchor storage), are never sent over the internet or to us, and are erased when you use Clear anchors or uninstall the game. When headsets play together, only the host's anchor identifier and where it stands in the room are shared, over your local network.
  • Line-up QR codes (co-op). If you choose to line up by a printed Barricaded QR code, your headset's own QR code tracking reads the code. The game is told only where the code is in your room and what it says (a link to our website with a letter). It never receives camera images, and nothing about the code is sent over the internet or to us; the hosting headset shares where the code is with the other headsets in the game over your local network. Your headset reads codes only while you are lining up by one, and it needs the game's Spatial Data permission.
  • Lining up by touch. Headsets can also line up by touching the same two spots; where the host touched them is shared with the other headsets over your local network.
  • Head and hand tracking are used only to play the game, on your headset. We don't record or keep them.
  • The game doesn't use your microphone, camera images, location or contacts.

Purchases and codes

Barricaded is free. It offers one optional in-app purchase, the Supporter Pack, sold through the Meta Horizon Store.

  • Buying. When you choose to buy it, the game opens Meta's own checkout. Meta takes the payment and keeps the record of the purchase, under Meta's terms and privacy policy. We never receive your card or other payment details.
  • Checking what you own. To unlock the pack's content, the game asks Meta's platform whether your account owns the Supporter Pack, and what it costs where you are (to show the price). It asks when the game starts, when you come back to it, and when you press CHECK AGAIN. Meta also keeps a copy of your purchases on your headset so the game can check them offline. The game doesn't send this anywhere.
  • Codes. We sometimes give away codes for the Supporter Pack. You redeem a code in Meta's own app, and Meta then lists the pack among your purchases. If you send us a message to get a code, we use your details only to send it to you.
  • Co-op. A game you host takes up to four players if you own the pack, and two if you don't. Your purchases aren't sent to the other headsets, but a player who can't join a full game is told how many it takes, and that more can play when the host has the Supporter Pack.

Playing at an arcade

Some VR arcades run Barricaded with their own arcade system and player accounts. If you sign up or sign in at an arcade, the arcade operator collects and looks after that account information, and the arcade's own privacy notice applies to it. We don't receive it.

Technical information

The game is built with the Unity engine, with Unity's engine statistics turned off. We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics services.

When you contact us

If you email us or use the support form on our website, we receive what you send: your message, the topic you chose, and, if you give them, your name, your email address and your headset model, plus the approximate country the message was sent from. The form's messages are delivered to our inbox through Cloudflare, which runs our website and email. We use them only to answer you and to fix problems, and keep them for as long as we need to for that.

This website

Our website (nightshift-games.com) is hosted by Cloudflare. It doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising. Like any website, Cloudflare's servers handle your IP address to deliver the pages, and the site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address when it delivers them.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone except as described above: Meta, which runs the leaderboards and the store (including purchases of the Supporter Pack); Cloudflare, which runs our score server, website and email; and other players, who can see leaderboard names, scores and stats. We would only disclose information if the law required it.

How long information is kept

  • Game information on your headset stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Leaderboard entries, and the standing our score server keeps for them, are kept for as long as the game's leaderboards exist, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your leaderboard entries. Email us from any address, with your Meta username, and we will delete your entries from the game's leaderboards and the standing our score server keeps.
  • See or correct your information. Your scores and stats are shown in the game. Email us with any question about the information we hold.
  • Uninstalling the game removes everything it saved on your headset.
  • You can manage your Meta account, username and privacy settings in Meta's own apps and settings.

We'll reply within 30 days.

Children

Barricaded is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game with an account, email us and we'll delete their leaderboard entries.

Security

Match results travel to our score server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and from there to Meta's leaderboard service. Our score server keeps only your leaderboard standing, described above.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using new information (for example online invites), we will update this policy before that happens and change the date at the top.
