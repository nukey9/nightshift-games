The game

Your room is the last safe place

Barricaded Mixed Reality is a wave-based zombie survival shooter built for mixed reality. It reads the room you're standing in and turns it into a fortress under siege: boarded-up windows and doors appear in your real walls, and the undead come through them.

The undead tear at the boards over your windows and smash through your walls. Nail planks back up, earn points for every kill, buy weapons off the wall and stock up on perks between rounds. Every wave comes harder, faster and hungrier than the last.

How long can you hold out?
