Play safely

Barricaded is a mixed reality game: you move around a real room while virtual zombies, barricades and weapons appear in it. Please read this before you play.

  • Clear your space. Set up your headset's room scan and boundary, and play in an open area free of furniture, pets, people, stairs, sharp edges and anything you could trip over or hit. Keep the space clear while you play.
  • Mind your surroundings. Virtual objects can look solid but aren't. Don't lean on, climb or walk into them, and don't swing your arms near walls, windows, lights or ceiling fans.
  • Outdoor play. Only play outside in a safe, level, private space that you are allowed to use, away from roads, water, drops and other people.
  • Co-op. When playing with others in the same space, give each other room to move and keep your play areas from overlapping.
  • Health. Take regular breaks. Stop at once if you feel dizzy, sick, disoriented or tired, or have eye strain or discomfort. Some people can have seizures or blackouts triggered by flashing lights or patterns; if you or your family have a history of epilepsy or seizures, talk to a doctor before playing.
  • Follow Meta's guidance. Read and follow the health and safety warnings that come with your Meta Quest headset.

You play at your own risk and are responsible for your own safety and your play space.
